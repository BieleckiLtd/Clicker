X TV · LIVE CAPTIONS

Connect your TV

Add captions to live streams with your own Groq account.

Start on your TV

Open Settings → Live captions on your TV, then scan its QR code. This page will connect to that TV.

1Get your Groq key

Sign in or create your free account, then choose Create API Key. Copy the key and return to this page.

Open Groq ↗

2Connect this TV

Your key is checked directly with Groq, then encrypted for your TV. The pairing service cannot read it. The QR code expires after 10 minutes.
Live transcription sends the stream’s audio to Groq using your account’s allowance. You can disconnect from TV Settings at any time.
✓

You’re connected

Your key has been sent to your TV. Leave the setup window open on the TV for a moment while it finishes connecting.

You can close this page.
